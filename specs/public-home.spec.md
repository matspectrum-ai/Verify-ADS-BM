# Spec: Public Home

## Route
`GET /`

## Acceptance criteria

1. The page exposes brand text `Verify Ads`.
2. The page exposes a navigation action to `/login` labeled `Minha Área`.
3. The hero contains the observed headline and explanatory copy.
4. `Acessar Minha Área` navigates to `/login`.
5. `Ver Benefícios` navigates to the benefits section on the same page.
6. Six observed benefit items are present.
7. Four observed metrics are present.
8. The three-step process is present in order.
9. Pricing exposes Starter, Business and Agency with the observed prices and domain limits.
10. Footer exposes links to `/l/privacidade`, `/l/termos` and `/login`.

## Verification boundary
Text/structure parity is implementable from current evidence. Pixel parity, typography, icons, animations and responsive parity remain unverified until a visual browser capture is available.
